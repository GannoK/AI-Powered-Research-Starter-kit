# Open this first

You do not need to be a professional researcher to use this kit.

The kit exists to stop a common failure mode:

> ask AI a broad question → receive a polished answer → mistake polish for evidence

Start with these rules:

1. AI is an assistant, not an authority.
2. Sources are not automatically evidence.
3. Evidence must support the exact claim being made.
4. Contradictory evidence must be preserved.
5. Uncertainty must not disappear during summarization.
6. A recommendation is not an accepted decision.
7. Search for what could prove you wrong.
8. Higher-consequence questions require stronger methods and often qualified human expertise.

For a new project, copy `02_RESEARCH_KNOWLEDGE_TEMPLATE/` into your own workspace and begin with `00_INDEX_AND_ROUTING.md`.
