# AI-Powered Research Starter Kit v0.1.0 — Public Preview

This is the first downloadable public preview of the AI-Powered Research Starter Kit.

The goal is simple: let people use AI to accelerate research **without confusing a fluent answer, a citation, or a search result with evidence**.

## Core principles

- **AI is not a source.**
- **A citation is not evidence until you verify what the source actually says.**
- **AI-generated research is a proposal or synthesis until the underlying evidence supports it.**
- **Do not ask only what supports your hypothesis. Ask what would prove it wrong.**

## Included

- beginner research guidance;
- three rigor profiles;
- source, evidence, and claims tracking;
- contradiction and uncertainty handling;
- falsification prompts;
- research-method selection using ADOPT → PROFILE → EXTEND → BUILD;
- AI/RAG research guidance;
- reusable prompts, templates, and checklists;
- explicit separation of evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and accepted decision;
- workflow installation guidance.

## Which download should I use?

**AI-Powered-Research-Workflow-Pack-v0.1.0.zip**

Use this if you simply want to drop the kit into your own AI or research workflow.

**AI-Powered-Research-Starter-Kit-v0.1.0.zip**

Use this if you want the complete repository contents for inspection, contribution, or reuse.

## Status

This is a **public preview**, not a claim that one generic workflow replaces formal discipline-specific research methods. A standards-reconciliation phase remains planned before 1.0.
