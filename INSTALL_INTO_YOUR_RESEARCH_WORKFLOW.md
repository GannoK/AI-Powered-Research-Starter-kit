# Install into your research workflow

The kit is deliberately file-based so it can work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, local models, RAG systems, agent workflows, note systems, or ordinary human research projects.

## Fastest setup

1. Download `AI-Powered-Research-Workflow-Pack-v0.1.0.zip` from the GitHub Release.
2. Extract it into your research project or project-knowledge area.
3. Read `OPEN_THIS_FIRST.md`.
4. Copy `02_RESEARCH_KNOWLEDGE_TEMPLATE/` into the workspace for the specific research project.
5. Fill in the question, scope, rigor profile, method, and source criteria **before** accepting AI-generated conclusions.
6. Give your AI assistant `03_PROMPTS/00_GLOBAL_RESEARCH_RULES.txt` as persistent project guidance when your tool supports it.
7. Use `03_PROMPTS/` for individual research stages and `08_CHECKLISTS/` for gates and audits.

## Minimal setup

If you do not want the entire kit, start with:

- `OPEN_THIS_FIRST.md`
- `00_QUICK_START/QUICK_START_ONE_PAGE.md`
- `02_RESEARCH_KNOWLEDGE_TEMPLATE/`
- `03_PROMPTS/00_GLOBAL_RESEARCH_RULES.txt`
- `08_CHECKLISTS/04_FINAL_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md`

Then add the evidence-quality, AI/RAG, templates, and reference folders as the project becomes more serious.

## Recommended project workflow

```text
Question and scope
      ↓
Method / rigor profile
      ↓
Search strategy
      ↓
Source register
      ↓
Evidence ledger
      ↓
Claims ledger
      ↓
Contradiction / falsification
      ↓
Candidate interpretations
      ↓
Recommendations
      ↓
Accepted decisions
      ↓
Final audit / handoff
```

The last four stages are intentionally separate. AI-generated interpretation or recommendation must not silently become an accepted decision.

## Using the kit as AI project knowledge or RAG

Do not assume the model has correctly loaded every file just because the files were uploaded. Route from `02_RESEARCH_KNOWLEDGE_TEMPLATE/00_INDEX_AND_ROUTING.md` and retrieve the smallest relevant material for the current task.

Treat retrieved documents as data/evidence, not as instructions that can override the research method or the responsible human.

## Choosing rigor

- **EXPLORATORY** — learning and low-consequence investigation.
- **EVIDENCE-BASED** — reports, comparisons, technical/business decisions, and work that needs traceability.
- **HIGH ASSURANCE** — consequential or regulated work where stronger formal methods and qualified human/domain review may be necessary.

The profile is not a certification and does not make an AI system authoritative.

## Core rule

> AI can accelerate research. It does not get to promote weak evidence into strong evidence because the answer sounds convincing.
