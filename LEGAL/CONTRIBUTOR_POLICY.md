# Contributor Policy

Status: active internal governance

## No automatic acceptance of IP terms

Opening an issue, receiving repository access, making a commit, submitting a pull request, delivering a document, participating in a chat, or being credited does not by itself transfer copyright ownership or create a royalty/revenue-share obligation.

Substantive contributions intended for incorporation into project-owned IP require an applicable signed employment, contractor, contributor, or assignment agreement before final acceptance.

## Required contributor representations

Before acceptance, the contributor must be able to represent that:
- they created the contribution or have authority to provide all necessary rights;
- the contribution does not knowingly infringe third-party rights;
- all third-party and background material is disclosed;
- applicable licenses permit the intended use;
- no undisclosed employer, client, publisher, confidentiality, or other obligation conflicts with the contribution;
- AI-assisted or generated material is disclosed as required by `AI_ASSISTED_AUTHORSHIP_POLICY.md`;
- any promised compensation, royalty, revenue share, equity, credit, or other consideration is documented separately and accurately.

## Ownership mechanism

The preferred contributor agreement uses:
1. work-made-for-hire treatment where legally available; and
2. a present fallback assignment of all assignable rights if work-made-for-hire treatment does not apply.

Do not rely on work-made-for-hire wording alone for commissioned work.

## Background IP

A contributor must identify any pre-existing material. No background IP may be embedded in a way that prevents authorized use, modification, commercialization, licensing, transfer, or maintenance contemplated by the applicable agreement.

## Compensation

Unless a separate signed agreement expressly states otherwise, a contribution does not create:
- royalties;
- revenue share;
- profit participation;
- equity;
- ownership of project IP;
- approval rights; or
- continuing control over exploitation of assigned work.

## Access and confidentiality

Repository access is limited to authorized purposes. Confidentiality duties must be created by the applicable signed agreement, NDA, employment duty, or other enforceable obligation; repository access alone is not treated as a substitute for an NDA.
