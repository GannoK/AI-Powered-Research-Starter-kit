# AI-Assisted Authorship and Provenance Policy

Status: active internal governance

## Purpose

AI assistance may accelerate research, drafting, coding, analysis, editing, and ideation. It must not erase provenance or create unsupported claims of authorship, ownership, or licensing.

## Classification

For material important enough to affect ownership, registration, commercialization, or enforcement, record the closest applicable category:
- human-authored;
- human-authored with AI assistance;
- AI-generated and materially selected/arranged/revised by a human;
- substantially machine-generated with limited human authorship;
- third-party material;
- licensed material;
- public-domain material.

## Required provenance where material

Record:
- human author/editor;
- date;
- tool/system used;
- relevant source inputs or reference material;
- material human selection, arrangement, editing, or transformation;
- third-party dependencies;
- final rights status;
- unresolved uncertainty.

Do not store credentials, private model secrets, or unnecessary personal data in provenance records.

## Research outputs

AI-generated research summaries are not evidence by themselves. Preserve the underlying source/evidence chain under the project's research methodology.

## Copyright and ownership claims

Do not describe purely machine-generated expression as exclusively human-authored. Where copyrightability matters, preserve evidence of meaningful human authorship and modification.

This policy does not guarantee copyrightability in any jurisdiction.

## Third-party and model terms

Before commercial or redistributed use of AI-assisted material, review material provider/tool terms when they materially affect:
- ownership;
- output-use rights;
- training/data restrictions;
- confidentiality;
- indemnity;
- attribution;
- prohibited uses;
- enterprise versus consumer terms.

## Sensitive inputs

Do not submit confidential, licensed, personal, regulated, or third-party restricted information to an AI system unless the applicable authorization and data-handling terms permit it.

## Review trigger

Revisit this policy when:
- governing law materially changes;
- material Copyright Office/court guidance changes;
- a project's AI tool or enterprise terms change;
- a publisher, customer, investor, or registration process imposes specific provenance requirements.
