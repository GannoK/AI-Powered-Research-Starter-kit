# Research lifecycle

1. Frame the question.
2. Define scope and terms.
3. Choose rigor profile.
4. ADOPT → PROFILE → EXTEND → BUILD the method.
5. Define source criteria.
6. Search and register sources.
7. Extract evidence.
8. Build claims.
9. Challenge/falsify.
10. Compare interpretations.
11. Form recommendations.
12. Record accepted decisions separately.
13. Audit and hand off.
