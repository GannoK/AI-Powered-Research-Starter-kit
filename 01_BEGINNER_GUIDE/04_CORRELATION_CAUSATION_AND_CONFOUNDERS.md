# Correlation, causation, and confounders

If two things occur together, one does not automatically cause the other.

Ask:

- Could A cause B?
- Could B cause A?
- Could C cause both?
- Could selection or measurement explain the pattern?
- Is the relationship observed, experimentally manipulated, or inferred?
- Does the study design justify causal language?

When the evidence supports only association, write association.
