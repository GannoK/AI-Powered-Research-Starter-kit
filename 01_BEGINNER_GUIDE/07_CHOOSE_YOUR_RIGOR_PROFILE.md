# Choose your rigor profile

## EXPLORATORY

Use for learning, orientation, brainstorming, and low-consequence questions.

Minimum:
- explicit question;
- basic source checking;
- uncertainty stated;
- no unsupported claims of certainty.

## EVIDENCE-BASED

Use for reports, decisions, technical comparisons, business research, serious explanatory work, or published material.

Add:
- documented search strategy;
- source register;
- evidence ledger;
- claims ledger;
- contradiction search;
- primary-source verification where material;
- provenance and limitations;
- reproducible handoff.

## HIGH ASSURANCE

Use when error could cause serious harm or the domain is regulated, safety-sensitive, medical, legal, policy-critical, scientific, or otherwise consequential.

Add:
- formal methodology;
- explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- stronger independent verification;
- reproducibility controls;
- appropriate statistical/methodological review;
- domain-expert involvement where needed;
- stricter evidence sufficiency thresholds.

Profiles are starting points, not certificates.
