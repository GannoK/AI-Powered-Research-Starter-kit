# Red flags: stop and check

Stop before accepting a conclusion when:

- the AI cannot produce the original source;
- a citation does not support the sentence;
- every source traces back to one article or study;
- evidence against the hypothesis is missing;
- key terms were never defined;
- causal language appears without causal evidence;
- only abstracts, snippets, or headlines were reviewed;
- a source is old in a fast-changing field;
- a source was corrected, withdrawn, or retracted;
- important assumptions are hidden;
- confidence is high but evidence quality is unclear;
- the conclusion matters enough that specialist review is appropriate.
