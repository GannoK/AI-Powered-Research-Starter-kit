# Source basics

A source is where information came from. Evidence is information from a source that is relevant to a particular claim.

Prefer original or primary material when the research question makes that meaningful: the paper, dataset, specification, court opinion, regulation, official record, source code, experiment output, or first-party documentation.

Secondary material can be valuable for explanation, discovery, context, and critique. It should not silently replace the original source when the original is available and material to the claim.

Source quality is contextual. Authority, recency, methodology, independence, conflicts of interest, and relevance all matter.
