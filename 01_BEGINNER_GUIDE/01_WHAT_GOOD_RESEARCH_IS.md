# What good research is

Good research is a controlled way of reducing uncertainty.

It does not mean collecting many links. It means defining a question, finding relevant information, deciding what counts as evidence, testing competing explanations, tracking uncertainty, and making conclusions no stronger than the evidence allows.

A good research record lets another person answer:

- What question was asked?
- What method was used?
- What sources were considered?
- Why were some sources trusted more than others?
- What evidence supports each important claim?
- What contradicts it?
- What assumptions remain?
- What would change the conclusion?
