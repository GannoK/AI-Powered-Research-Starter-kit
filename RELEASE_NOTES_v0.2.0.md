# Release Notes — v0.2.0

Date: 2026-09-08

## Purpose

v0.2.0 establishes a proprietary intellectual-property and licensing baseline for current distributions of the AI-Powered Research Starter Kit while preserving the validity and provenance of the historical v0.1.0 CC BY 4.0 release.

## Methodology

The research methodology itself remains unchanged in this release:
- AI is not a source;
- claims must trace to evidence and sources;
- contradictory evidence is preserved;
- evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and accepted decision remain separate;
- method selection remains ADOPT → PROFILE → EXTEND → BUILD.

## Legal review

The included agreement templates require completion and review by qualified counsel before execution. Repository files alone do not create a signed contributor, commercial, royalty, or confidentiality agreement.
