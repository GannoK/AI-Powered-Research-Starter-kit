# Method selection

Default rule:

**ADOPT → PROFILE → EXTEND → BUILD**

Do not invent a method because a custom method feels more tailored.

BUILD is last resort. Record the mandatory property that mature approaches fail to satisfy and the evidence supporting that conclusion.

For formal or high-assurance work, identify the mature standards, protocols, reporting guidelines, or disciplinary methods that govern the specific field and verify their current versions from authoritative sources.
